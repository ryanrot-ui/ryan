Our Story

Where Every Dish Tells a Story

Cocha is a Chinese-Korean fusion restaurant nestled in the heart of Shin-Okubo, Tokyo's vibrant Korean district. We blend the bold, communal spirit of Korean BBQ with the depth and variety of Chinese flavors — creating a dining experience unlike anything else in the neighborhood.

Our menu spans straw-grilled samgyeopsal, haemul jeongol hot pots, yukhoe tartare, and more — served in a modern, upscale space with great music, friendly staff, and an all-you-can-drink option perfect for groups who like to take their time.
